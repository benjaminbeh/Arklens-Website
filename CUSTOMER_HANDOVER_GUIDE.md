# Your Website, Your Choice: Handover Guide

## Your Domain Always Belongs to You

At Arklens, we believe you should never be locked into a service. **Your domain name is yours**, your business content is yours, and you're always free to move if your needs change.

The principle is simple:

| Belongs to you | Belongs to Arklens |
|----------------|--------------------|
| Your domain name | Website source code |
| Your business information and text | Templates and reusable components |
| Your logos, photos and media | Design systems and development frameworks |
| Your business data and customer data | Automation scripts, deployment systems and internal tools |

**Your business assets are yours. Our technology remains ours.** You are free to leave at any time, and we help you do it. The Free Website Programme simply does not include a transfer of Arklens source code.

This guide explains what happens if you decide to leave Arklens, if the Free Website Programme ends, or if we can no longer provide free hosting for your website.

---

## When Does Handover Apply?

Handover becomes relevant in these situations:

1. **You choose to leave Arklens** for any reason
2. **The Free Website Programme ends** (with 60 days' notice)
3. **Your free hosting is discontinued** (with 60 days' notice, except in cases of abuse or security issues)
4. **Arklens ceases operations** (unlikely, but covered)

In all cases, you have clear options and we're here to help.

---

## What You Receive: the Customer Asset Package

Whenever you leave (Options B and C below), you receive a **Customer Asset Package** at no charge. It contains everything that belongs to you:

- ✅ Your business text and content (services, opening hours, contact details, descriptions)
- ✅ Your logos, photos, images and other media you provided
- ✅ Business and customer data we hold for you (for example, form submissions)
- ✅ A list of your page addresses, so your new setup can keep links and search visibility
- ✅ Your current DNS settings and clear DNS instructions

Not included automatically:

- ❌ Arklens source code, templates, reusable components and design systems (including the compiled website build)
- ❌ Arklens development frameworks, automation scripts, deployment systems and internal tools
- ❌ Other customers' code or data
- ❌ Access to Arklens Cloudflare accounts

**Need the source code?** If you would like to keep running the existing website exactly as it is, Arklens may offer a separate **Source Code Buyout** or **Migration Package**, depending on the website and its complexity. This is agreed in writing before any work begins and may involve a fee.

## Your Three Options

### Option A: Continue with Arklens (Paid Service)

**What it means:** Keep everything as it is, but move to a paid plan.

**When available:** If Arklens offers a paid hosting or maintenance plan at the time of transition.

**What you get:**
- Your website stays online without interruption
- Same team managing your website
- All your content, images, and setup remain unchanged
- Ongoing updates and support

**What it costs:**
- We'll provide clear pricing before you need to decide
- No charges begin without your explicit written agreement
- Typically: CHF 20-50/month depending on services

**How to choose this:**
- We'll email you with pricing details
- Reply to confirm you'd like to continue
- We'll set up billing and everything continues smoothly

✅ **Best for:** Businesses that are happy with Arklens and want continuity with minimal effort.

---

### Option B: Move to Your Own Infrastructure

**What it means:** You (or someone you hire) run your online presence on hosting you control.

**When to choose this:** You have technical capability, or a trusted developer, and want full control of hosting.

**What you get from Arklens:**
- Your Customer Asset Package (see above)
- Help connecting your domain to your new hosting
- SSL and DNS guidance
- Up to 2 hours of handover support via email or video call

**Two ways to do it:**
1. **Rebuild with your content:** your developer builds a new site using your Customer Asset Package. No Arklens code is needed.
2. **Keep the existing Arklens-built site:** requires a separately agreed Source Code Buyout or Migration Package.

**What you need:**
- Your own hosting account (for example Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or a traditional host)
- A developer to build or deploy the site, unless you have the skills yourself
- Your domain login credentials (you should already have these)

**Timeline:**
- Customer Asset Package prepared: 1-2 business days
- Your new setup: depends on your developer, typically 1-4 weeks

✅ **Best for:** Businesses with technical resources or a trusted developer who want full control.

---

### Option C: Move to Another Provider

**What it means:** Take your online presence to a different web design or hosting company.

**When to choose this:** You want a different provider to manage everything for you.

**What you get from Arklens:**
- Your Customer Asset Package, in standard formats any provider can work with (text, image files, CSV/JSON data)
- Up to 1 hour of handover support to answer questions
- Available to speak with your new provider if needed

Your new provider can build on your content in their preferred system. Arklens source code, templates and reusable components are not automatically included; a Source Code Buyout or Migration Package can be discussed separately.

**What you need:**
- A new web design or hosting provider

**Estimated cost:**
- New provider setup: set by your new provider
- Ongoing hosting: varies by provider

**Timeline:**
- Customer Asset Package prepared: 1-2 business days
- Your new provider's timeline: varies (typically 1-4 weeks)

✅ **Best for:** Businesses that want a managed service but prefer a different provider.

## Your Domain: Always Under Your Control

Regardless of which option you choose:

### If You Manage Your Domain (Recommended)

✅ **You're already in control.** Simply update your DNS settings to point to your new hosting.

**Steps:**
1. Log in to your domain registrar
2. Update DNS records as instructed by new hosting
3. Wait 5-60 minutes for changes to propagate
4. Your website now points to new hosting

**We'll provide:**
- Clear instructions for DNS updates
- Support if you get stuck
- Confirmation when old hosting can be safely removed

### If Arklens Manages Your Domain (Optional Setup)

If you chose to have Arklens manage your DNS settings:

✅ **We'll transfer control back to you immediately.**

**Steps:**
1. We provide current DNS settings (for your records)
2. We update nameservers at your registrar to point back to you
3. OR we transfer domain management to your account
4. You have full control within 24-48 hours

**Important:** The domain registration is always in your name. We only manage DNS settings if you specifically requested this.

## Handover Timeline

### Notice Period

**If we're ending service:**
- Minimum 60 days' written notice (usually email)
- Except in cases of abuse, security threats, or Terms violations
- Notice period starts when we send official notification

**If you're leaving:**
- No notice period required
- Request handover anytime
- We aim to respond within 2 business days

### Handover Process

**Week 1:**
- You request handover or we notify you of service changes
- We discuss your chosen option (A, B, or C)
- We answer questions, including whether a Source Code Buyout or Migration Package makes sense for you

**Week 2-3:**
- We prepare your Customer Asset Package (Options B & C)
- We provide DNS documentation
- We schedule a handover support call if needed

**Week 4-8:**
- You complete the transition to your new setup
- Your domain points to the new hosting
- We remain available for questions during the notice period

**After Transition:**
- Old hosting deactivated (after confirmation)
- Domain fully under your control
- Handover complete

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get my content anytime?

Yes. Even if you're staying with Arklens, you can request a copy of your Customer Asset Package at any time. Just email support@arklens.ch.

### Do I own my website?

You always own your domain name, business information, customer data and any logos, photos or content you provide. Arklens retains ownership of its source code, templates, reusable components, design systems, automation scripts and development tools unless a separate written agreement transfers those rights.

### Can I take the source code with me?

The standard Free Website Programme does not include ownership or transfer of Arklens source code. If you need it, we may offer a separate Source Code Buyout or Migration Package depending on the website and its complexity.

### What if I don't have technical skills?

**Option A** (stay with a paid plan) or **Option C** (move to another managed provider) are designed for non-technical customers.

### Will my website go down during transition?

**Option A:** No downtime at all.

**Options B & C:** Brief downtime is possible during the DNS changeover. With planning, many transitions have zero downtime by setting up the new site before switching DNS.

### What if I lose access to my domain?

Contact your domain registrar immediately. They can verify your identity and restore access. We can provide documentation showing you're the owner if needed.

### Can I come back to Arklens later?

If you leave and later want to return, contact us. Depending on capacity, we may be able to re-onboard you (likely on a paid plan rather than the free programme).

### What happens if Arklens shuts down?

Unlikely, but covered: we'll provide 60 days' notice if possible, and all customers receive their Customer Asset Package automatically. Your domain remains yours. In that situation we will also discuss what can be done to keep existing websites running.
