# Customer Ownership Guide

**What You Own, What We Manage**

---

## The Core Principle

**You own your business and domain. Arklens manages the technical infrastructure while providing the service.**

**Domain + business content = you. Arklens technology + source code = Arklens.**

This means:
- ✅ You keep full control of your digital assets
- ✅ You can leave or migrate anytime
- ✅ Arklens handles all technical complexity
- ✅ Your domain, content and data are never held back
- ℹ️ Arklens source code and templates stay with Arklens unless separately agreed

---

## What You Own

### Your Domain Name
- **You own it** - Your domain (e.g., restaurant-example.ch) always belongs to you
- **Your registrar account** - You maintain the account with your domain registrar (Infomaniak, GoDaddy, etc.)
- **Your renewal payments** - You pay your registrar directly for domain renewal
- **Your control** - You can transfer your domain to another provider at any time

**Arklens never:**
- Purchases domains on your behalf (unless explicitly agreed)
- Controls your registrar account
- Holds your domain hostage
- Charges domain fees

### Your Business Content
- **Text and copy** - All written content on your website
- **Images and media** - Your photos, logos, videos
- **Brand assets you provide** - Your logo, brand colours and brand guidelines
- **Business information** - Your services, prices, contact details

**You can:**
- Export all content at any time
- Use content on other platforms
- Update content through Arklens (per your service agreement)
- Receive all your content files if you leave

### Your Business Data
- **Customer inquiries** - Contact form submissions
- **Booking data** - Appointments, reservations
- **Analytics data** - Website traffic and visitor information
- **Customer reviews** - Reviews and testimonials

**Data rights:**
- Request data export at any time
- Receive data in standard formats (JSON, CSV, SQL)
- Delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- Take data with you if you migrate

### Your Email Accounts
- **Business email** - Your business email service (if separate from website hosting)
- **Email provider account** - Your relationship with email provider
- **Email data** - All your email messages and contacts

**Note:** Email is typically separate from website hosting. Arklens can help set up email, but you own the service and data.

### Your External Subscriptions
- **Payment processors** - Your Stripe, PayPal, or other payment accounts
- **Booking systems** - Your Calendly, Acuity, or other booking tools
- **Marketing tools** - Your Mailchimp, newsletter, or CRM accounts
- **Analytics** - Your Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel accounts

**Unless explicitly included in your Arklens service agreement**, you own and manage these directly.

## What Arklens Manages (While You're Our Customer)

### Website Hosting
- **Cloudflare Pages hosting** - Your website runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure
- **Uptime and reliability** - We monitor and maintain hosting
- **Server infrastructure** - We handle all server-side technical details
- **Backups** - We maintain website code and configuration backups

**You don't need to:**
- Understand how hosting works
- Manage servers or infrastructure
- Worry about uptime or performance
- Handle technical maintenance

### Technical Deployment
- **Building your website** - Converting your design and content into a live website
- **Deploying updates** - Pushing changes to your live site
- **Version management** - Tracking changes and maintaining versions
- **Rollback capability** - Restoring previous versions if needed

**Arklens handles:**
- All technical build processes
- Deployment pipeline and automation
- Testing and quality assurance
- Version control and change tracking

### Domain Configuration
- **DNS setup** - Connecting your domain to your website
- **DNS management** - Managing technical DNS records (if you delegate this to us)
- **SSL certificates** - Automatic HTTPS security certificates
- **Certificate renewal** - Automatic renewal, no expiration issues

**Two models:**

**Model 1: You manage DNS (Recommended)**
- You keep full control at your registrar
- We provide DNS records to configure
- You point your domain to our infrastructure
- You can change this anytime

**Model 2: Arklens manages DNS**
- You delegate nameservers to Arklens (via Cloudflare)
- We manage all DNS records for you
- You can reclaim DNS control anytime by changing nameservers back
- You still own the domain at your registrar

### SSL/TLS Security
- **HTTPS encryption** - Your website is always secure
- **Automatic certificates** - No manual certificate management
- **Automatic renewal** - Certificates never expire
- **Security updates** - We apply security patches

**You get:**
- Padlock icon in browser
- Secure customer connections
- SEO benefits of HTTPS
- Peace of mind

### Website Maintenance
- **Content updates** - Making changes to your website (per your service agreement)
- **Bug fixes** - Fixing technical issues
- **Security patches** - Keeping software up to date
- **Performance optimization** - Ensuring fast load times

**Scope depends on your service agreement:**
- Free Website Programme: Basic maintenance included
- Paid services: Maintenance scope defined in agreement
- On-demand changes: Billed separately or per agreement

**Need the source code?** Arklens may offer a separate Source Code Buyout or Migration Package, depending on the website and its complexity. This is agreed in writing and may involve a fee.

## What You Do NOT Get

### No Cloudflare Account Access

**You will NOT receive:**
- Login credentials to Arklens' Cloudflare account
- Access to Cloudflare Pages dashboard
- Access to Workers or deployment controls
- Access to DNS management interface

**Why?**
1. **Security** - Prevents accidental configuration that could break your site
2. **Separation** - Protects you from seeing other customers' websites or data
3. **Simplicity** - You shouldn't need to understand Cloudflare's technical complexity
4. **Support** - Arklens maintains clear responsibility for infrastructure

**Cloudflare is infrastructure, not a customer control panel.**

Think of it like electricity:
- You use electricity in your business
- You pay your electricity bill
- You don't need access to the power plant

Similarly:
- Your website runs on Cloudflare
- Arklens manages the technical infrastructure
- You don't need Cloudflare account access

### No Direct Deployment Controls

**You cannot:**
- Deploy changes yourself via Cloudflare
- Access build pipelines or automation
- Configure Workers or Pages directly
- Modify DNS settings in Cloudflare (unless using separate DNS management)

**Instead:**
- Request changes through Arklens
- We deploy updates per your service agreement
- We handle all technical complexity
- You receive a working website

## If You Decide to Leave Arklens

### You Take With You

**1. Your Domain (Already Yours)**
- No transfer needed - you already own it
- Simply point it to your new hosting provider
- Update DNS records at your registrar
- Your domain never stops working

**2. Your Customer Asset Package**

Arklens provides within 7 days, at no charge:
- ✅ All your text and content
- ✅ All your images, logos and media
- ✅ All business data (forms, bookings, etc.)
- ✅ A list of your page addresses (for redirects and search visibility)
- ✅ Current DNS settings and instructions

**Delivery format:**
- ZIP file in standard formats (text, image files, CSV/JSON)
- Short documentation for your new provider

**Not included automatically:** Arklens source code, templates, reusable components and the compiled website build. A Source Code Buyout or Migration Package can be agreed separately if you want to keep running the existing site as it is.

**3. Technical Support During Transition**

Arklens provides reasonable assistance:
- Explanation of how your website is structured (pages, features, integrations)
- Guidance on DNS reconfiguration
- Recommendations for new hosting
- Documentation of technical requirements

**Arklens does NOT:**
- Perform the migration for you
- Set up your new hosting
- Provide ongoing support after handover
- Guarantee compatibility with new provider

### Migration Process

**Step 1: You decide to migrate**
- Contact Arklens with migration date
- We prepare your handover package
- Review what's included

**Step 2: Arklens provides your Customer Asset Package**
- All content and data
- Your images and media
- Page address list
- DNS configuration info

**Step 3: You or your new provider:**
- Set up new hosting
- Build or deploy your new website using your content
- Configure your domain DNS
- Test new setup

**Step 4: DNS switchover**
- Update DNS at your registrar
- Point to new hosting
- DNS propagation (24-48 hours)
- Old hosting remains active during transition

**Step 5: Confirm and close**
- Verify new site is working
- Arklens decommissions old hosting
- Service agreement ends

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get access to the Cloudflare account?

No. Cloudflare is infrastructure that Arklens manages. You don't need (or want) to understand Cloudflare's technical complexity. 

If you need changes, request them through Arklens. If you want full technical control, you'll need to migrate to your own infrastructure.

### What if I want to move my website to another provider?

You can, anytime. You already own your domain. Arklens will provide your Customer Asset Package (content, images and data) within 7 days of request, and reasonable help with the move. Arklens source code, templates and reusable components are not automatically included in a migration.

### Do I need my own Cloudflare account?

No. Arklens manages Cloudflare infrastructure. If you leave Arklens and want to self-host on Cloudflare, you can set up your own account and deploy a website there. Arklens provides your Customer Asset Package; deploying the existing Arklens-built site requires a separate Source Code Buyout or Migration Package.

### Can I export my content while staying with Arklens?

Yes. You can request a content export at any time for backup or peace of mind. Your content is never locked in.

### What happens to my domain if Arklens closes?

Your domain is already in your control at your registrar. If Arklens closes, you simply point your domain to new hosting. Your domain registrar and domain ownership are completely independent of Arklens.

### Who pays for my domain?

You do, directly to your domain registrar. Arklens never charges for domain registration or renewal (unless explicitly agreed as part of a service package, which would be clearly stated).

### Can Arklens take my domain?

No. Arklens never owns, controls, or has access to your domain registrar account. Your domain is legally yours.

### What if I want to keep my domain but use different hosting?

That's the point! Your domain belongs to you. Change DNS records at your registrar to point to any hosting provider you choose. Arklens will provide your Customer Asset Package.

### How long does DNS migration take?

DNS changes typically propagate within 24-48 hours. During this time, some visitors see the old site, some see the new site. There's no downtime, just gradual switchover.

### What data does Arklens keep after I leave?

Per our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy:
- We delete your content and business data
- We may retain anonymized usage statistics
- We retain transaction records for accounting (per legal requirements)
- You receive your Customer Asset Package before deletion

### Can I take my email with me?

If your email is managed separately (e.g., Google Workspace, Microsoft 365), it's already yours - nothing to migrate.

If email is hosted with your website, Arklens provides email export and configuration details.

### Is there a fee for migrating away from Arklens?

No. You receive your Customer Asset Package and reasonable migration assistance as part of your service. No exit fee, no handover fee, no data export fee. A Source Code Buyout or Migration Package is optional and quoted separately.

### Can I take the source code with me?

The standard Free Website Programme does not include ownership or transfer of Arklens source code. If you require it, Arklens may offer a separate Source Code Buyout or Migration Package depending on the website and its complexity.

### Can my web agency or IT provider use Arklens to build something for free?

The Free Website Programme is intended to help eligible businesses improve their own digital presence. It may not be used by agencies, freelancers, IT providers or other third parties to obtain free development work for resale, white-labelling or incorporation into their own paid services.
